# Teplotni rovnovaha v polovodici

Nasledujici tabulka byla vyrobena chatem:
| Abstract mechanism                                              | Physical effector                               | Typical examples                              | Effect on (pn)       | Approximate relationship                                                         |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Thermal equilibrium (reference state)**                       | $T$                                             | Uniform semiconductor at constant temperature | $pn = n_i^2(T)$      | $pn \propto n_i(T)^2,\quad n_i \propto T^{3/2} e^{-E_g/(2kT)}$                   |
| **Temperature change (equilibrium maintained)**                 | $T$                                             | Heating or cooling after reaching equilibrium | $pn = n_i^2(T)$      | $pn \propto T^3 e^{-E_g/(kT)}$                                                   |
| **Electric field / applied voltage causing carrier injection**  | $E,V$                                           | Forward-biased PN junction, ohmic injection   | $pn > n_i^2$         | $pn = n_i^2 e^{qV/(kT)}$ (low-level injection, ideal junction)                   |
| **Electric field / applied voltage causing carrier extraction** | $E,V$                                           | Reverse-biased PN junction, depletion         | $pn < n_i^2$         | No universal law; carrier concentrations decrease below equilibrium values       |
| **Photon absorption (optical generation)**                      | Photon flux $\Phi$, optical power $P_{\rm opt}$ | Photodiode, solar cell                        | $pn > n_i^2$         | $\Delta n = \Delta p \propto \Phi$ (or absorbed optical power)                   |
| **Ionizing radiation generation**                               | Radiation flux $\Psi$ or dose rate $D$          | X-rays, $\gamma$-rays, particle detectors     | $pn > n_i^2$         | $\Delta n = \Delta p \propto \Psi$ (approximately)                               |
| **Impact ionization (avalanche multiplication)**                | High electric field $E$                         | Avalanche diode, breakdown region             | $pn > n_i^2$         | Generation rate $G \propto \alpha(E)$, where $\alpha$ increases rapidly with $E$ |
| **Carrier generation/recombination by external sources**        | Generation rate $G$, recombination rate $R$     | Optical pumping, electrical excitation        | $pn \gtrless n_i^2$  | $pn$ increases when $G>R$, decreases when $G<R$                                  |
| **Uniform doping change (new equilibrium)**                     | Dopant concentration $N_D, N_A$                 | Different donor/acceptor levels               | $pn = n_i^2$         | $n_0 \approx N_D,; p_0 \approx n_i^2/N_D$ (n-type), product unchanged            |
| **Non-uniform doping (after equilibrium established)**          | Dopant gradient $\nabla N_D, \nabla N_A$        | Graded junctions                              | Locally $pn = n_i^2$ | Built-in field compensates diffusion in equilibrium                              |
